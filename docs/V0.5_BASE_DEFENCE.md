# v0.5 — Base defence and sieges

## Player loop

1. Gather wood and build a workbench.
2. Craft reinforced barricades and wooden spike traps.
3. Use the HUD warning to prepare the area around the base.
4. Survive three waves of zombies and goblins.
5. Repair the layout and prepare for the next siege.

Natural sieges occur once every seven complete day-and-night cycles. A complete
cycle lasts twenty minutes, so the normal interval is 140 minutes of active
Survival play. World time and the next siege boundary are saved with checksum and
recovery data. They pause when no living Survival player is present, so an
unattended server does not consume cycles or trigger an attack. The 45-second HUD
warning begins when the seventh cycle is completed.

## Defences

- **Reinforced Barricade:** crafted in pairs from six planks and two sticks at a
  workbench. It uses a narrow wall collider and has more than twice the breach
  resistance of a normal wooden wall.
- **Wooden Spike Trap:** crafted in pairs from four planks and four sticks at a
  workbench. It is non-colliding, damages zombies and goblins twice per second,
  and sharply reduces their horizontal movement while they stand on it.

Raid enemies can breach reinforced barricades, normal wooden walls, plank blocks
and log walls. Partial breach damage slowly recovers after attackers move away.
Other building materials are not silently destroyed by this first siege version.

## Waves and multiplayer

The server owns all timers, spawning, targeting, trap damage and block damage.
Wave size scales with the number of living Survival players and is capped at 32
pending enemies. Enemies spawn 24–36 blocks from a selected player on loaded,
safe terrain and are assigned a player target immediately.

The HUD displays the warning countdown, wave number, remaining attackers and
intermission timer. `/siege status` also reports how many full cycles remain before
the next natural siege. Creative players do not count toward siege scaling or
world-cycle progression.

Administrators can use:

- `/siege status` to inspect the current phase;
- `/siege start` to begin the warning immediately for testing.

## Compatibility

The two v0.5 block IDs are appended after every existing block ID. v0.4 worlds,
chunks, players and inventories therefore keep their existing numeric layout.
Worlds containing v0.5 defence blocks should not be opened in older builds.
