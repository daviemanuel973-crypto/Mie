# v0.8 — Difficulty and Hardcore

This is the first playable milestone promoted from the Survival roadmap after
the v0.7.2 stabilization release.

## World creation

New worlds choose one of four server-authoritative difficulties:

| Difficulty | Incoming damage | Starvation floor | Natural sieges | Siege size |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Peaceful | 50% | disabled | disabled | 65% for manual sieges |
| Easy | 75% | 20 HP | enabled | 75% |
| Normal | 100% | 10 HP | enabled | 100% |
| Hard | 150% | 0 HP | enabled | 135% |

Hardcore always locks the selected difficulty to Hard. A dead Hardcore
character remains dead and the server rejects respawn requests. This is a
gameplay lock rather than destructive deletion: the world and player save stay
available for backup, inspection and future spectator work.

## Persistence and multiplayer

- The world root stores a small versioned `worldDifficulty` payload.
- Existing worlds without the payload load as Normal and non-Hardcore.
- Invalid payloads fail closed to the same compatible defaults.
- The server sends the sanitized rules to each joining client.
- Damage, starvation, scheduled sieges and respawn decisions are made by the
  server; hiding a client button is not the security boundary.

## Scope

This milestone does not yet add day/night spawn pressure, item durability,
furnace progress UI, recipe discovery, farming expansion or beds. Those remain
separate roadmap slices so each can ship with save, multiplayer and Low-preset
validation rather than as untested placeholders.
