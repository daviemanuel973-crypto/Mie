# Mie Survival v0.10.0 — subsistence expansion

v0.10.0 is the first content cycle after the v0.9 stabilization line. The
initial milestone strengthens the survival loop with useful exploration loot,
root crops and prepared food built on systems already present in the game.

## First playable slice

- Barn chests can contain carrots; igloo chests can contain potatoes.
- Hold Shift and right-click dirt or grass to plant wheat, strawberries, chilli,
  carrots or potatoes. Planting and harvesting are authoritative in multiplayer
  and persist across chunk unloads and restarts.
- Mature carrot plots return two carrots; mature potato plots return three
  potatoes. Their growth times are six minutes and six-and-a-half minutes.
- A low-poly campfire can be crafted at a workbench from planks,
  cobblestone and charcoal. It is the dedicated station for baked potatoes.
- Cooking pots prepare vegetable stew and berry porridge.
- Prepared meals restore more hunger than raw ingredients. Stew and porridge
  also grant a bounded Saturated effect.

## Compatibility contracts

- Persisted item IDs remain append-only. v0.10 owns IDs 2194 through 2198.
- Persisted block IDs 0 through 211 remain frozen. Crop blocks use 212 through
  216 and the campfire uses 217.
- The 108 recipe indexes shipped by v0.9 remain unchanged; new recipes use
  indexes 108 through 111.
- Recipe discovery format 3 uses a 47-byte bit set. Format 1 payloads from v0.9
  and the early format 2 payload are remapped so their item bits remain attached
  to the same stable item IDs after six block IDs are appended.
- Farm-plot format 1 remains valid because its crop enum was extended without
  changing existing numeric values.
- Multiplayer protocol 5 prevents older clients from interpreting the expanded
  block registry and the new planting action with an incompatible packet layout.

## Art status

Carrot, potato, baked potato, vegetable stew and berry porridge have distinct
pixel-art inventory icons. Mature crop textures are used for the five inexpensive
cross-mesh crop blocks, while the campfire uses a static crossed-plane model to
keep its rendering cost low on integrated graphics.

## Next 0.10 milestones

The first subsistence slice is feature-complete. Corrective optimization and
post-release regressions are reserved for v0.10.1, while blocking defects found
by the v0.10.0 release gates will still be fixed before publication.
