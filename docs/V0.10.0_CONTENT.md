# Mie Survival v0.10.0 — subsistence expansion

v0.10.0 is the first content cycle after the v0.9 stabilization line. The
initial milestone strengthens the survival loop with useful exploration loot,
root crops and prepared food built on systems already present in the game.

## First playable slice

- Barn chests can contain carrots; igloo chests can contain potatoes.
- Carrots and potatoes can be eaten directly or planted through the persistent
  farm-plot API.
- Mature carrot plots return two carrots; mature potato plots return three
  potatoes. Their growth times are six minutes and six-and-a-half minutes.
- Cooking pots prepare baked potatoes, vegetable stew and berry porridge.
- Prepared meals restore more hunger than raw ingredients. Stew and porridge
  also grant a bounded Saturated effect.

## Compatibility contracts

- Persisted item IDs remain append-only. v0.10 owns IDs 2194 through 2198.
- The 108 recipe indexes shipped by v0.9 remain unchanged; new recipes use
  indexes 108 through 110.
- Recipe discovery format 2 uses a 46-byte bit set. Format 1 payloads from v0.9
  are accepted, copied intact and zero-filled for the new content.
- Farm-plot format 1 remains valid because its crop enum was extended without
  changing existing numeric values.
- Multiplayer protocol 4 prevents older clients from interpreting the expanded
  item and recipe registries.

## Art status

Carrot and potato inventory icons are derived from the existing crop textures.
Prepared food temporarily reuses cooking-pot artwork; dedicated meal icons are
planned before v0.10.0 is promoted from development to a release candidate.

## Next 0.10 milestones

The content cycle can continue with live planting interactions, a dedicated
campfire cooking station, additional survival recipes and presentation polish.
Corrective optimization and post-release regressions are reserved for v0.10.1,
while blocking defects found by the v0.10.0 release gates will still be fixed
before publication.
