# v0.8 — Day/night spawn pressure

This is the second playable Survival roadmap milestone in the v0.8 development
line. It uses the existing persistent world clock and server-owned entity system
instead of creating a parallel spawn simulation.

## Rules

- Daylight keeps the existing passive pig, cat and neutral-goblin ecology.
- Passive ecology slows down and uses half its daytime per-player budget at night.
- Natural hostile spawning is limited to zombies and only runs at night.
- Natural hostiles spawn 22–46 blocks from a living Survival player and remain at
  least 18 blocks from every connected player.
- Only loaded, simulated, solid and dry surface terrain is eligible.
- A siege warning, wave or intermission suspends natural hostile spawning so the
  two pressure systems do not stack.
- Peaceful never creates natural hostiles. Spawn eggs and explicit administrator
  actions remain available.

## Bounded pressure

The server computes a hostile cap from living Survival players and difficulty:

| Difficulty | Base cap per player | Initial attempt interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Peaceful | 0 | disabled |
| Easy | 3 | 13–18 seconds |
| Normal | 5 | 9–14 seconds |
| Hard / Hardcore | 7 | 6–10 seconds |

Every three visible days adds one hostile slot and shortens attempts by half a
second. This age pressure is capped after four steps, and the whole simulated
world has a hard ceiling of 48 natural hostiles. Failed terrain searches do not
loop without a bound.

Existing zombies, including spawn-egg or administrator-created zombies, count
towards the cap. Zombies already present at sunrise remain until defeated, but
no new natural hostile is created during the day.

## Multiplayer and visibility

The server owns the clock, cap, target selection, terrain validation and entity
creation. Clients only receive normal authoritative entity updates. The pause
menu reports calm, night pressure or an active siege, and the read-only
`/pressure` command exposes the current natural-hostile count and cap.
